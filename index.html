Bio

Hello! I'm currently a PhD candidate in Electrical & Computer Engineering at New York University, fortunate to be advised by Prof. Wenqi Cui. I obtained my MSc in Statistics from Renmin University of China, under the guidance of Prof. Hanfang Yang and Prof. Hongteng Xu. I was also a visiting research intern at Carnegie Mellon University, co-hosted by Prof. Shixiang (Woody) Zhu and Prof. Holly Wiberg. My research interests lie in machine learning for sequential data modeling and sequential decision making. Specifically, I focus on reinforcement learning, stochastic process, control theory, and their applications in cyber-physical energy systems.
